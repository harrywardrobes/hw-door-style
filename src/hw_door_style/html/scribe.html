What is the maximum ceiling height of the wardrobes? (mm)
Width (left-right) x length (into the room), mm. Sizes the floor and ceiling; the ceiling moves to the ceiling height.
Back wall to the back of the facing (mm): standard / bridge units + worktops. Set on every unit (finished depth adds the facing / door).
Plinth height under the units (mm)
Drawer front height (mm) - every drawer unit; the box is 78 mm less. The linen press follows its mid-rail.
Runouts (scribe allowance)
Units with their own height
